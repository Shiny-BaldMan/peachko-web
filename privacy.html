Privacy Policy

At PEACHKO, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our K-Beauty matching services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal identification information from Users in various ways, including:

  • Identity Data: Name, nationality, and date of birth provided during sign-up.
  • Contact Data: Email address and phone number for communication.
  • Booking Data: Hotel addresses in Seoul, preferred artists, and specific service requests.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and usage patterns via Firebase Analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To process and manage your beauty service reservations.
  • To facilitate communication between you and your assigned artist.
  • To verify your identity and ensure a secure transaction environment.
  • To improve our platform's user experience based on feedback and usage trends.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. Your information is shared only with the specific artist you have booked to ensure they can provide the service at your location. All data is securely stored using Google Firebase, which adheres to global security standards.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal information at any time. To exercise these rights, please contact our support team or manage your profile settings.

5. Consent

By using our service, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. We may update this policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons.
