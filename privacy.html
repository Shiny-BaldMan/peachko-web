Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Welcome to PEACHKO. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you visit our website and tell you about your privacy rights.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with our on-demand K-Beauty services, we may collect, use, and store the following types of personal data:

  • Identity Data: First name, last name, date of birth, and nationality.
  • Contact Data: Email address and telephone number.
  • Booking Data: Hotel name, address in Seoul, reservation dates, and specific styling requests.

2. How We Use Your Information

We will only use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To register you as a new customer and manage your account.
  • To process and deliver your beauty service bookings.
  • To communicate with you regarding your appointment (e.g., artist arrival notifications).

3. Data Security

We have put in place appropriate security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used, or accessed in an unauthorized way. Your data is securely stored using Google Firebase, which complies with strict international security standards.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. Your booking information (Name, Hotel Address, specific requests) is only shared with your assigned makeup artist to ensure they can provide the requested service.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or our privacy practices, please contact our support team via our official KakaoTalk or WhatsApp channels provided on the website.
